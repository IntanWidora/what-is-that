SECTION 3 & 4

WHAT IS THAT?

We use What is that? to ask about one thing that is further away.

Grammar Key

What = Apa

That = Itu

Question Formula:

What is that?

Answer Formula:

That is a [object].

👉 📦

"What is that?"

"That is a bag."

🗣️ Let's Practice Saying The Sentences!

SECTION 5

WHEN TO USE A OR AN?

We use A or AN before ONE single thing!

🅰️

Use "A"

When the word starts with a consonant sound (p, b, r, s, t, c...)

✏️
A pencil
📚
A book
📏
A ruler
🎒
A bag
🔤

Use "AN"

When the word starts with a vowel sound (A, E, I, O, U)

🧹
AN eraser

Eraser starts with 'E'!

🍎
AN apple

Apple starts with 'A'!

💡 GOLDEN REMINDER

A pencil, BUT AN eraser!

SECTION 9, 10 & 11

HOW MANY? (NUMBERS 1 - 20)

We use How many ...? to ask about the number of things.

⭐ Special Plural Rule: Add "-s"!

When there is more than one thing, add an -s at the end!

✏️

1 Pencil

One pencil

✏️✏️

2 Pencils

Two pencils

✏️✏️✏️

3 Pencils
